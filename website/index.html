FREQUENTLY ASKED

Got questions?
We've got answers.

Do I need to create an account or sign up?

No. Releases are directly accessible .apk and .exe files without signing up or creating an account. Download and launch immediately.

What connection method should I use?

USB gives you the absolute lowest latency (<2ms) and is recommended for competitive gaming. Wi-Fi is wireless and works great for casual gaming on the same router. Bluetooth PAN works when both USB and Wi-Fi are unavailable — just enable Bluetooth Tethering on your phone.

Will using the phone touchpad freeze my keyboard or mouse?

No. GyroPad registers as a separate virtual device. Your physical keyboard and mouse continue to work normally alongside GyroPad's touchpad input. Windows merges the inputs automatically, just like having two mice plugged in at once.

Does it work with all games?

GyroPad emulates a standard Xbox 360 controller via ViGEmBus. Any game that supports XInput or DirectInput controllers will recognise it automatically — including Steam games, emulators, and most AAA titles. In-game button remapping also applies to GyroPad.

Is the app safe? Can others on my network connect?

Yes, it's safe. Each session generates a fresh 4-digit PIN displayed only on your phone screen. Anyone attempting to connect must enter that PIN before the stream starts. The source code is fully open on GitHub for inspection.
