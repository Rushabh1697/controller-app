What you get

Everything a controller needs — on your phone

GyroPad gives your phone the full layout of a modern console controller, plus gyroscope motion input that no physical controller can match.

🕹️

Full Button Layout

D-pad, face buttons, shoulder buttons, triggers, dual thumbsticks, touchpad, share and options — all on your screen.

🌀

Gyroscope Aiming

Tilt your phone to aim — the gyroscope tracks every movement with precision. Perfect for shooters and racing games.

🏎️

Racing / Tilt Steering

Hold your phone sideways like a steering wheel. GyroPad maps the tilt directly to analogue steering in F1 and other racing games.

🖱️

Touchpad as Mouse

Slide your finger across the touchpad area to move your PC mouse cursor — great for navigating game menus.

🔀

Custom Button Mapping

Reassign any button to anything. Save your layout so it's ready every time you launch.

🔒

Secure PIN Pairing

A unique 4-digit PIN is shown on your phone each session. Only your PC can connect — no open ports, no risk.

Setup guide

Up and running in 5 minutes

Follow these steps in order. You only need to do this once.

⚠️ Before you begin — you need one extra thing

GyroPad needs a free Windows driver called ViGEmBus to create a virtual controller that your games can see. Without it, the app connects but games won't detect any input.

↗
Download ViGEmBus from github.com/nefarius/ViGEmBus/releases — look for the latest ViGEmBus_Setup_x.x.x.exe file.
↗
Run the installer and restart your PC once. That's the only setup needed.

  1. Find your phone's IP address Go to Settings → Wi-Fi → tap your network name → look for "IP address." It looks like 192.168.1.XX.
  2. Type that IP into the GyroPadHost window Paste or type the IP address into the IP field in the app on your PC.
  3. Enter the PIN from your phone Type the 4-digit PIN shown on your phone screen into the PIN field.
  4. Click "Start Controller" The status on your phone switches to green: CONNECTED. You're ready to play.
  1. Pair your phone with your PC via Bluetooth On your phone, go to Settings → Bluetooth and pair with your PC the normal way.
  2. Turn on Bluetooth Tethering on your phone Settings → Network & internet → Hotspot & tethering → turn on Bluetooth tethering.
  3. Connect to your phone's Bluetooth network on your PC In Windows system tray → Bluetooth → connect to your phone's network access point.
  4. Open Command Prompt and run: GyroPadHost-Windows.exe --bluetooth --gui — then enter the PIN when prompted.
  1. Enable Developer Options on your phone Settings → About phone → tap Build number 7 times quickly. You'll see "Developer mode enabled."

Open your game and play

Once your phone shows CONNECTED, Windows sees a new Xbox 360 controller. Open any game, go to its controller settings, and it should be detected automatically — no configuration needed on the game side.

CONNECTED — controller active
🏎️ For racing games: hold your phone horizontally (landscape) before connecting, and select the Landscape profile in GyroPadHost. Tilt it like a steering wheel.
🎯 For shooters: use the Calibrate Neutral button in GyroPadHost while holding your phone in your natural resting position, then small tilts will translate to precise gyro aim.
🔀 Click Edit Mapping to remap any button to any input — your layout is saved automatically.
Questions

Common questions

Do I need a GitHub account to download GyroPad?
No. The download links on this page go directly to the files. Just click and download — no sign-in, no account, nothing to create.
Does it work over Wi-Fi, or do I need a cable?
Wi-Fi is the easiest and the recommended way for most people — both devices just need to be on the same home network. If you want the absolute lowest latency or don't have shared Wi-Fi, USB cable mode is available too. Bluetooth is also supported for wireless play without shared Wi-Fi.
Do I need to root my phone or do anything complicated?
No. GyroPad only needs the sensors and network access that every Android phone has out of the box. No root, no developer mode needed for Wi-Fi mode, no modifications to your phone at all.
Will my game actually detect it as a real controller?
Yes. GyroPad uses a Windows driver called ViGEmBus to create a virtual Xbox 360 controller. Windows and games see it exactly the same as a physical gamepad plugged in — if a game supports controllers, it supports GyroPad.
Is my connection secure? Can someone else connect to my phone?
Yes, it's secure. Every time you open the GyroPad app a new random 4-digit PIN is generated. Only a PC that enters the correct PIN can connect. The connection only happens over your local network — nothing goes to the internet.
My phone isn't being detected / authentication failed. What do I do?
Most likely the PIN was entered wrong, or the app was restarted (which changes the PIN). Close and reopen the GyroPad app on your phone to see the current PIN, then re-enter it on the PC. Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network. If using USB mode, make sure you accepted the "Allow USB debugging?" prompt on your phone.
The game is not detecting any input even though the app says CONNECTED.
ViGEmBus is probably not installed. Download and install it from github.com/nefarius/ViGEmBus/releases, restart your PC, and try again. GyroPad warns you in the log if ViGEmBus is missing.
